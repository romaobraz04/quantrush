Public beta checklist

    Progress backup

    Export your saved sessions or import a QuantRush backup. Existing sessions are kept.

    Your data

    QuantRush stores arithmetic attempts, session dates, answers, timing, settings, and calculated progress. The readiness index is a training estimate, not a pass probability.

    Guest and account progress

    Guest history and preferences stay in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes them. When you sign in, guest sessions are added to that account and synced with Supabase. Account history is kept until you request its deletion. Backups contain training history, not passwords or sign-in tokens.

    Services

    Supabase handles accounts and saved history. The site uses GitHub Pages during the hosting transition and Cloudflare Pages after migration. jsDelivr delivers the Supabase client code. These providers process connection information to deliver the app. When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile performs security checks on account forms. Their service logs may have separate retention periods.

    Feedback

    The linked Google Form is optional. Its responses and charts are private to the QuantRush owner. You may include a reply email, but it is not collected automatically. There are no ads or marketing trackers in this beta.

    Access and deletion

    Use Progress backup in Account to download your locally available history. Sync first to include all account sessions. For access, correction, or deletion requests, use the account/data-request category in the feedback form. Account ownership will be verified before a deletion; submitting a form does not delete anything automatically.
